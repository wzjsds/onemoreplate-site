One tap per set.

Changing the weight, the reps or the exercise is there when you need it, and out of the way when you don’t.

04 · The reasons

Every weight comes with its reason.

Next time follows your plan and what you actually lifted. Hit your reps and the weight goes up; fall short and it holds.

Ask why any weight is what it is, and the app shows the rule behind it, in plain words.

Why 87.5 kg

All 3×5 done last time, so the weight goes up.

Three sets of 5 at one weight. When all 15 reps are done, it goes up 2.5 kg next time. A set short of 5, or lighter than planned, keeps the weight; two sessions like that in a row take it down 10% to build back up.
