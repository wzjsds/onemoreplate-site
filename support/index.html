Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: email support@onemoreplate.app. We usually reply within a few days.

It helps to include your iPhone model, the iOS version, and the app version (Me → About).

Please don't attach your export or backup files, or health details. Your training stays on your phone, and we'd like to keep it that way.

Refunds for One More Plate Pro go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
