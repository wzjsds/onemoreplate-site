English

Effective 17 October 2026

The short version. One More Plate keeps your training on your iPhone. We run no servers, there are no accounts, and the app sends nothing on its own. Your data moves only when you move it: to Apple Health if you turn that on, to Files when you save a backup there, and wherever you share an export, a card or text. With Pro and the app on your Apple Watch, a workout you start also runs as a workout on the watch, and Apple Health records your heart rate during it (section 4). We can’t see your workouts.

1. Who we are

One More Plate (加一片) is made by Zijie Wu, an individual developer in Glasgow, United Kingdom (“we”). Contact: support@onemoreplate.app.

We are based in the UK. We have not appointed a representative in the EU, because we process personal data only occasionally, when you email us, and not on a large scale (GDPR Art. 27(2)(a)).

2. What the app keeps, and where

Everything below stays on your device, in the app’s own storage:

  • your workouts: exercises, sets, weights, reps, effort ratings, how your joints and muscles felt at the start if you answered, notes and times;
  • your plans and how far you are through them;
  • the training profile you fill in, such as your goal, experience, training days and session length, and optionally your sex and estimated maxes;
  • body measurements you choose to log, such as body weight and body fat;
  • exercises you create, and your settings;
  • backups of your data, as files in the app’s own storage: a daily one while “Auto backup” is on, any you make with “Back up now”, and a safety copy the app makes before each import. The app keeps the newest seven of these, of any kind, and deletes older ones;
  • short-lived copies the app makes while you save a backup to Files, export a spreadsheet, share a card or import a file. Each is deleted once it has been used, and any left behind are deleted when you delete your data in the app.

Heart rate is not on this list: with an Apple Watch (Pro), the app shows your heart rate live during a workout and on its summary afterwards, but never stores it (section 4).

None of it is sent to us. The app contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it makes no network requests of its own.

3. When data leaves the app

Only when you send it:

  • Export as a spreadsheet creates a CSV file, and you choose where it goes through the iOS share sheet.
  • Share cards are images you choose to share.
  • Ask your own AI puts a summary of your recent training into text that you share through the iOS share sheet. Once you paste it into an AI service, that service’s terms and privacy policy apply, not ours.
  • Save backup to Files saves a backup of your data as a file in the place you choose in Files. If that place is iCloud Drive or another storage provider, the file is kept under Apple’s or that provider’s terms, not ours. You can import it back into the app.
  • Also copy to a folder, if you turn it on, puts a copy of each day’s backup in a folder you pick in Files and keeps the newest seven there. The same terms apply as for Save backup to Files. The app deletes only its own older copies in that folder, never anything else.
  • Apple Health, if you turn it on, receives each workout you finish: its start and end time, or, for a workout your Apple Watch recorded (Pro), the watch’s workout with its heart rate and active energy (section 4). With Pro and the app on your watch, the watch runs a workout session for each workout whether saving is on or off, and Health records your heart rate during it; the switch decides only whether the workout is saved to Health.
  • Your iPhone’s own backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup made on a computer) may include the app’s storage. Those backups are made under Apple’s terms and your device settings, not ours.

When you bring a file in:

  • Import from Files lets you pick a backup you saved in Files. The app first saves a safety copy of your current data, then copies the file you pick, merges it into your data, and deletes the copy; the original stays where it was.
  • Import from Strong or Hevy lets you pick that app’s CSV export in Files. The app copies the file you pick, reads it, and deletes the copy; the original stays where it was. Nothing is sent anywhere.

4. Apple Health

Saving to Apple Health is off unless you turn on “Save workouts to Apple Health” (Profile → Connections & data). Then each workout you finish is saved to Health as Traditional Strength Training, with only its start and end time: no sets, weights, calories or distance. A workout your Apple Watch recorded is saved as the watch recorded it instead, with its heart rate and active energy (below). Health also records which app and device wrote it, and the app adds a random workout ID. A workout ends at your last logged set, and one with no logged set isn’t saved. The app reads only heart rate and workouts from Health, and only for the Apple Watch features below. Health is Apple’s store on your device (and in iCloud if you sync Health there); we never receive any of it. We never use Health data for advertising, marketing or data mining, and never sell or share it. Turn saving off with the same switch or in the Health app. Workouts already saved stay in Health until you delete them there.

Apple Watch and heart rate (Pro). If One More Plate is on your paired Apple Watch, a workout you start on your iPhone also starts a workout session on the watch, whether “Save workouts to Apple Health” is on or off (the switch decides only whether the workout is saved to Health), and Apple’s HealthKit records your heart rate and active energy during it, as for any watch workout. The first time, iOS asks on the watch and on the iPhone whether the app may read your heart rate. While you train, the watch passes its latest heart rate straight to your iPhone to show on both screens; it isn’t kept.

  • What is written. With “Save workouts to Apple Health” on, the workout saved to Health is the one the watch recorded, instead of the iPhone’s entry with only a start and end time. Either way, one workout is saved, not two. With the switch off, the watch throws its workout away and the app saves nothing, though the heart-rate readings the watch took may stay in Health, as Apple keeps them.
  • What is read. Only two things: the heart rate Health holds from a finished workout’s start to its finish (at most 12 hours), and only for a workout a watch recorded; and workouts, only to check whether the watch already saved this one. Reading heart rate doesn’t need the Save switch.
  • Where it shows. The workout summary shows your average and highest heart rate and a heart-rate curve. They are read from Health each time they are shown, and heart rate is never stored in the app, its backups or its exports. The app keeps only a list of which workouts a watch recorded (workout IDs, no readings), on this iPhone only and never in a backup or export.
  • Share images. The curve and your average and highest heart rate appear on a workout’s share image only when you share it and leave its “Heart rate” switch on (it is on each time the preview opens).
  • Your choice. Change the permission at any time in the Health app (your profile → Apps → One More Plate) or in iOS Settings. Without it, the watch still shows and logs your sets; no heart rate is shown.

Heart rate and other Health data never reach us: there is no server, and the app sends nothing over the internet. We never use them for advertising, marketing or data mining, never sell them, and never share them with third parties. They are used only to show your training back to you. A heart rate leaves the app only on a share image you send yourself.

5. Notifications and Live Activities

Rest-timer alerts are scheduled on your device. The workout shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island comes from data on your device. There is no push server.

Refunds go through Apple. Your statutory rights are not affected.

The app may ask iOS to show its rating prompt; iOS decides whether it appears. Ratings and reviews go to Apple, not through us. The app only records, on your device, when it last asked.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Delete. “Delete training and personal data” (Profile → Connections & data → Backup, import & privacy) removes your workouts, plans, templates, training profile, body measurements, custom exercises, and the backups saved in the app. Your language and theme stay, and so does the saved Pro record, so Pro stays unlocked. It also turns off “Save workouts to Apple Health”; workouts already saved to Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. Deleting the app removes everything in its own storage. It also stops “Also copy to a folder”. Backups you saved to Files or that were copied to your folder, and files you exported or shared elsewhere, are yours and are not touched.
  • Your training data never reaches us, so we cannot access, correct or delete it for you. It is under your control on your device.
  • Emails you sent us: you can ask to see, correct or delete them, to restrict how we use them, or to get a copy in a portable format. You can object to how we use them, and withdraw any consent you gave. You can also complain to your data-protection authority.

For people in California: we don’t sell or share personal information, as California law defines those words. The app itself sends us nothing, and nothing in it tracks you across other apps or websites, so a Do Not Track signal has nothing to switch off; the app doesn’t look for one.

For people in Washington State: your training and body measurements stay on your device and never reach us, so we don’t collect consumer health data through the app.

10. Children

One More Plate is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn a child under 13 has emailed us, we delete it.

11. Health

One More Plate is a training aid, not medical advice. The measurements and training data you enter stay on your device (section 2) unless you send them yourself (sections 3 and 4).

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change would mean the app starts collecting data, we will tell you in the app before it happens.

1. 我们是谁

加一片（One More Plate）由独立开发者 Zijie Wu（英国格拉斯哥）开发，下称“我们”。联系方式：support@onemoreplate.app。

2. App 保存什么、存在哪里

以下内容都只存在你的设备上，存放在 App 自己的存储空间里：

  • 训练记录：动作、组数、重量、次数、主观用力程度、开练时关节和肌肉的感觉（如果你回答了）、备注和时间；
  • 你的训练计划及其进度；
  • 你填写的训练资料，如训练目标、训练年限、每周训练天数和每次时长，以及可选填写的性别和预估最大重量；

  • 你创建的自定义动作，以及各项设置；
  • 数据备份，以文件形式存放在 App 自己的存储空间里：打开“自动备份”时每天一份，你用“立即备份”做的备份，以及每次导入前 App 自动存的安全副本。这几类合在一起只保留最近 7 份，更早的会被删除；

心率不在上面的清单里：使用 Apple Watch（Pro）时，App 会在训练中实时显示心率，并在练完后的总结页显示，但从不保存（见第 4 条）。

以上内容都不会发送给我们。App 里没有任何统计分析、广告、追踪或崩溃上报代码，App 自身也不发起任何网络请求。

3. 数据什么时候会离开 App

只在你自己发出时：

  • 导出为表格：生成一个 CSV 文件，通过系统分享面板，由你决定发到哪里。
  • 分享卡片：你选择分享的图片。
  • 问问你自己的 AI：把你最近训练的摘要整理成一段文字，由你通过系统分享面板发出。你把它粘贴到某个 AI 服务后，适用的是该服务的条款和隐私政策，而不是本政策。
  • 将备份存到“文件”：把你的数据备份成一个文件，存到你在“文件”App 里选择的位置。如果你选的是 iCloud 云盘或其他存储服务，这个文件就依据 Apple 或该服务商的条款保存，与我们无关。之后你可以把它导入回 App。
  • 同时复制到文件夹：如果你打开它，App 每天会把当天的备份再复制一份到你在“文件”里选的文件夹，并在那里保留最近 7 份。适用的条款与“将备份存到‘文件’”相同。App 只会删除它自己在那个文件夹里存的较早副本，不会动其他任何文件。
  • “健康”：如果你打开了写入，“健康”会收到你练完的每次训练：它的开始和结束时间；如果这次训练由你的 Apple Watch 记录（Pro），则是手表记录的这次训练，包含心率和活动能量（见第 4 条）。如果你有 Pro 且手表上装有本 App，无论写入开关是否打开，手表都会为每次训练运行一个体能训练会话，“健康”会记录期间的心率；开关只决定这次训练是否存入“健康”。
  • iPhone 自身的备份：iCloud 云备份或电脑备份可能包含 App 的存储内容。这些备份依据 Apple 的条款和你的设备设置进行，与我们无关。

从其他 App 导入文件时：

  • 从“文件”导入：你在“文件”里选择之前存好的备份。App 会先为你当前的数据存一份安全副本，再复制你选的文件、把它合并进你的数据，然后删除这份副本，原文件仍留在原处。

4. “健康”

写入“健康”默认关闭，只有你在“我的 → 连接与数据”中打开“把训练写入‘健康’”后才会开启。打开后，你练完的每次训练都会以“传统力量训练”存入“健康”，只包含开始和结束时间，不含组数、重量、卡路里或距离；由 Apple Watch 记录的训练则按手表记录的样子存入，包含心率和活动能量（见下文）。“健康”还会记录写入它的 App 和设备，App 也会附上一个随机的训练编号。训练的结束时间是你记录最后一组的时间；一组都没有记录的训练不会写入。App 只从“健康”读取心率和训练记录，而且只用于下文的 Apple Watch 功能。“健康”是 Apple 在你设备上的数据存储（如果你开启了“健康”的 iCloud 同步，也会存到 iCloud）；我们从不接收其中的任何数据。我们绝不把“健康”数据用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，也绝不出售或共享这些数据。你可以用同一个开关停止写入，也可以在“健康”App 里关闭。已经存入“健康”的训练会一直保留，直到你在“健康”里删除。

Apple Watch 与心率（Pro）。 如果你配对的 Apple Watch 上装有加一片，你在 iPhone 上开始训练时，无论“把训练写入‘健康’”是否打开，手表都会为这次训练开始一个体能训练会话（开关只决定这次训练是否存入“健康”），由 Apple 的 HealthKit 像记录其他手表训练一样记录你的心率和活动能量。第一次时，iOS 会在手表和 iPhone 上分别询问是否允许 App 读取你的心率。训练中，手表会把最新的心率直接传给你的 iPhone，在两边屏幕上显示，不会保存。

  • 写入什么： 打开“把训练写入‘健康’”时，存入“健康”的是手表记录的这次训练，取代 iPhone 那条只含开始和结束时间的记录；无论哪种，都只存一次，不会存两次。关闭时，手表会丢弃它记录的训练，App 不写入任何内容；但手表测得的心率读数可能仍按 Apple 的方式留在“健康”里。
  • 读取什么： 只有两样：已结束的训练从开始到结束这段时间（最长 12 小时）里“健康”记录的心率，而且只针对手表记录过的训练；以及训练记录，只用来确认手表是否已经存过这次训练。读取心率不需要打开写入开关。
  • 在哪里显示： 练完后的总结页显示平均心率、最高心率和心率曲线。每次显示时都从“健康”读取，心率从不存入 App、App 的备份或导出文件。App 只记一份“哪些训练由手表记录”的清单（只有训练编号，没有读数），只留在这台 iPhone 上，不会进入备份或导出文件。
  • 分享图： 只有当你分享某次训练、并让它的“心率”开关保持打开（每次打开预览时默认打开）时，分享图上才会出现心率曲线和平均、最高心率。
  • 你的选择： 你可以随时在“健康”App（轻点头像 →“App”→“加一片”）或 iOS“设置”里更改这项权限。 关闭后，手表照样显示和记录你的每一组，只是不再显示心率。

心率和其他“健康”数据从不会发给我们：我们没有服务器，App 也不通过网络发送任何内容。我们绝不把它们用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，绝不出售，也绝不与任何第三方共享；它们只用于把你的训练显示给你自己。心率离开 App 的唯一途径，是你自己发出的分享图。
